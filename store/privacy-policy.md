# Play Time Privacy Policy

_Effective October 12, 2026_

Play Time is a sideline tool for youth soccer coaches. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

## What Play Time stores

You can enter team names, player first and last names, optional nicknames, jersey numbers, game settings, and the minutes each player played. This information is stored only on your device, inside the app's private storage.

## What Play Time collects

Nothing. Play Time has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, and no analytics. It doesn't send your data to us or to anyone else, and it makes no network requests while you use it.

## Sharing

Data leaves your device only when you choose **Export backup** in Settings. The backup file goes wherever you send it through your device's share sheet. Anything you do with that file is up to you.

## Children

Play Time is intended for coaches, not for children to use. Coaches may enter the names of the players they coach. That information stays on the coach's device as described above.

## Deleting your data

Delete a team, player, or game inside the app, or use **Settings → Clear all data**. Uninstalling Play Time also removes everything it stored.
